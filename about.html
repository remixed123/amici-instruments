The Little Story Behind Amici Instruments

Amici Instruments started with our son, Dante.

Dante is autistic and has been fascinated by music, rhythm and sound from a very young age. He also has a wonderfully direct way of interacting with technology — if something makes sense, feels interesting and is fun to explore, he dives straight in.

That gave Dad an idea.

Glenn originally set out to build a simple little music app just for Dante. In fact, in its earliest days, the project itself was called “Dante.”

Of course, as often happens with Dad projects, “simple” didn't stay simple for very long.

As Glenn kept designing, experimenting and adding ideas, he realised that many of the things he wanted for Dante — an interface that felt immediate, playful, visual and inviting — could make music creation more enjoyable for everyone.

That little project gradually grew into Marco and Bruno, and somewhere along the way, Amici Instruments was born.

Three Highly Specialised Departments

Today, Amici Instruments is a tiny family business.

Glenn

Product Guy, Designer & Developer

Glenn is the person behind the instruments. He designs, develops and endlessly experiments with new ways to make powerful music tools feel intuitive and fun.

Emily

Marketing & Voice of Reason

Emily looks after the marketing side of Amici, and helps with internationalisation — particularly across Asian markets.

Dante

Chief Usability Tester

Dante is the original reason Amici exists and remains our most uncompromising usability tester. His testing methodology is refreshingly simple: if something is interesting, intuitive and fun, he uses it. If it isn't, he moves on.

Designing something for Dante also taught us something that has stayed at the heart of Amici Instruments:

A musical instrument shouldn't need to explain itself before it invites you to play.

That idea shapes the instruments we make today.

We want our apps to have depth without feeling complicated — instruments that encourage you to touch things, experiment, make unexpected sounds and enjoy discovering what happens next.

Marco and Bruno may now be professional music-making tools, but underneath all the oscillators, filters and modulations, there is still a little bit of that original project called Dante.

Three people. Two instruments. Lots of ideas. And it all started with Dante.
