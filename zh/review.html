给中文校对者

你只需要判断一件事：中文读起来自不自然。

技术内容是否正确，由 Glenn 对照左边的英文原文来判断，不需要你操心。你不需要懂合成器。

  1. 只看右边的中文。哪一句读着别扭、生硬、像机器翻译，或者一个真正的中文母语者不会这么说，就标出来。
  2. 句子中夹杂的英文单词（Dual Hard Sync、Fold、Master 等）是故意保留的——它们是 App 界面上的实际按钮名称，App 界面本身是英文的。请不要建议翻译它们，但如果某句话因为夹了英文而读不通，请告诉我们。
  3. 橙色标注的行是我们自己也拿不准的地方，特别希望听到你的意见。
  4. 语气应该是：亲切、自信、给音乐人看的。不是官方公文，也不是电商促销。

不确定的地方，请直接写下你会怎么说。

For Glenn

You check the technical side; she checks the Chinese. Neither of you has to cover for the other.

  • Read the English on the left. If a Chinese line was meant to say something it doesn't, you'll usually catch it from the terminology row in the glossary rather than the prose.
  • The orange rows are genuine judgement calls I made. They're where a wrong choice would cost the most, so they're worth your five minutes.
  • Every English UI label is kept verbatim inside the Chinese sentences. That's deliberate: your app UI is English, so a Chinese user reading 「点一下 Dual Hard Sync 按钮」 goes looking for a button that actually exists.
  • Brand names, wordmarks (MARCO / BRUNO), engine names (Analog, FM, Fold, Resonant) and voice names (Kick, Snare…) are untranslated throughout, with a Chinese gloss on first use.
